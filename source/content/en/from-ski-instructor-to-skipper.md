People sometimes ask how someone from Zagreb, far from the sea, ends up as a skipper. For me it went through the mountains first.

## Skiing first

I've been skiing my whole life. It became more than a hobby, and I became a licensed ski instructor with an ISIA licence. Teaching skiing taught me something I still use every day on a boat: people relax and learn best when they trust the person in charge and are having fun at the same time.

## Kinesiology, and then the sea

I studied at the Faculty of Kinesiology at the University of Zagreb, where I specialised in sailing and water sports. That's where I discovered sailing, and I was hooked almost immediately.

What followed was a lot of time on the water:

- sailing small boat classes like the Laser Radial, Optimist and Laser Stratos
- windsurfing
- working as a sailing instructor for the Faculty of Kinesiology on Murter
- racing in regattas
- sailing big boats, 45 feet and up, all over the Adriatic

## Becoming a skipper

At some point the hobby became a profession. Since 2020 I've worked as a captain and skipper for private guests and charters, on monohulls like the Bénéteau First 45, Bavaria 46 and Dufour 46, and on catamarans like the Lagoon 42, Lagoon 450 and Lagoon 46.

In 2024 I completed the Sail Week skipper academy course with an A grade. I hold a Croatian boat licence (category C).

## What I bring on board

- **Knowledge of the Adriatic weather.** Reading bura, jugo and maestral is the core of the job. I wrote about it in [this guide to Adriatic winds](/blog/adriatic-winds-bura-jugo-maestral/).
- **More than ten years of organising sports activities**, from ski lessons to windsurfing, fitness and sailing. Keeping a group safe, busy and happy is familiar work.
- **Three languages.** Croatian is my mother tongue, I speak English at C1 level and German at B1.
- **A calm, cheerful way of working with people.** That matters as much as seamanship when you share a boat for a week.

## Beyond sailing

In winter I'm back on the slopes, and I also build websites and apps, including this one. This blog will focus on sailing for now, with skiing posts to follow.
