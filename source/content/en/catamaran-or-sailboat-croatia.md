I skipper both. Recently that has meant monohulls like the Bénéteau First 45, Bavaria 46 and Dufour 46, and catamarans like the Lagoon 42, Lagoon 450 and Lagoon 46. Guests often ask which one I'd choose. It depends on the group.

## Space and living on board

Catamarans win clearly here. The saloon and cockpit are on one level, the trampoline at the front becomes the most popular place on board, and the cabins are spread across two hulls, which gives more privacy.

On a monohull of 45 to 46 feet, living space is good, but everything is more compact and the cabins are closer together.

## Stability

A catamaran doesn't heel. That matters for people who get seasick, for families with small children and for anyone who wants to cook or read while sailing. At anchor, a catamaran rolls much less when a swell comes into the bay.

A monohull heels under sail. For many people that is exactly the fun of it, but some guests find it uncomfortable at first.

## The feel of sailing

If you want to feel the boat come alive, choose a monohull. It points higher into the wind, responds more directly to the helm and sails well in the moderate breezes that are typical of an Adriatic summer. A performance-oriented cruiser like the First 45 is a joy in a good maestral.

A cruising catamaran sails well off the wind, but it doesn't point as high, and in light air many charter catamarans end up motoring more.

## Marinas and anchorages

Monohulls fit into more marina berths and are simpler to moor stern-to. Catamarans are wide, so in peak season some marinas have limited space for them, and prices are often higher.

On the other hand, a catamaran's shallow draft means you can anchor closer to the beach in many bays.

## Cost

For a comparable size and season, a catamaran usually costs noticeably more to charter than a monohull. Fuel costs are also higher, because there are two engines.

## My recommendation

- **Families, larger groups, first-time sailors, people prone to seasickness:** catamaran.
- **Couples and friends who want to sail, smaller budgets, people who love the feeling of heel:** monohull.
- **Mixed groups:** a catamaran is the safer choice for keeping everyone happy.
