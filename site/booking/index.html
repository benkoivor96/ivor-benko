Sailboat or catamaran?

Read the full comparison →

Sailboat

  • Feels more like sailing, with heel and speed under sail
  • Usually the lower price
  • Smaller cabins and less deck space
  • A good choice for sailing lovers and smaller groups

Catamaran

  • Spacious and stable, with a big shared cockpit and deck
  • Cabins are similar in size and often have their own bathrooms
  • Usually the higher price
  • A good choice for families and groups who want space

Questions about booking

When is the best time to sail in Croatia?

July and August are the hottest and busiest months. June and September are often milder with fewer boats, and the sea is still warm for swimming. I'm happy to advise on what suits your group.

How early should I book?

As early as you can, especially for July and August, because the most popular boats and bases fill up first.

Do I need a skipper?

If nobody in your group holds a boat licence recognised in Croatia and a VHF radio certificate, yes. Many groups with licences hire one anyway. I wrote about it in the blog post on whether you need a skipper.

Sailboat or catamaran, which should I take?

It depends on your group and what you want from the week. The comparison above is a start, and I'm glad to give you a recommendation if you tell me about your group.

Can you help me prepare for the trip?

Yes. I wrote a packing list for a week of sailing in Croatia, and I'm happy to answer questions before you arrive.
