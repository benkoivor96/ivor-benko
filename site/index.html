Skipper

What I do on board

As your skipper I'm responsible for the boat and for everyone on it. I read the forecast every morning and evening and choose the route and tonight's anchorage for tomorrow's wind. I handle the check-in with the charter base, every marina and every mooring, and I'm happy to teach anyone who wants to steer, trim or learn the knots.

I learned to sail at the Faculty of Kinesiology in Zagreb, worked as a sailing instructor on Murter and have been skippering since 2020.
