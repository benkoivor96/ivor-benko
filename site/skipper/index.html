What you get with me as your skipper

  • A route built around the weather. Bura, jugo and maestral decide where it's safe and pleasant to be. I check the forecast every morning and evening.
  • Marinas, buoys and anchoring handled. Mooring stern-to in a busy July marina is my job, not yours.
  • Check-in and check-out with the charter base. Inventory, engine, sails and damage report, so there are no surprises at the end.
  • Local knowledge. Which bay is sheltered from tonight's wind, where you can tie up for dinner, where the water is clearest in the morning.
  • Sailing lessons if you want them. I've worked as a sailing instructor. Steering, sail trim, knots and mooring, at whatever pace suits you.
  • Three languages. Croatian, English (C1) and German (B1).

Where I sail

The whole Croatian Adriatic, from Istria and Kvarner to central and southern Dalmatia. I know the Kornati and the Šibenik archipelago especially well, because I worked as a sailing instructor on Murter.

Questions guests ask

Do I need a skipper to charter a boat in Croatia?

If nobody in your group has a boat licence recognised in Croatia and a VHF radio certificate, yes. Even with licences, many groups hire a skipper so that everyone can relax. I wrote more about it in the blog post on whether you need a skipper.

Which boats do you skipper?

Monohull sailboats like the Bénéteau First 45, Bavaria 46, Dufour 46 and catamarans like the Lagoon 42, Lagoon 450, Lagoon 46. Similar boats of that size are fine too.

Can you teach us to sail during the week?

Yes. I've worked as a sailing instructor, and I'm happy to teach steering, sail trim, knots and mooring to anyone who wants to learn.

Which languages do you speak?

Croatian (native), English (C1) and German (B1).

Where does the skipper sleep?

Usually in a separate cabin or in the skipper's cabin that many catamarans have in the bow. We agree on this before you choose the boat.

What does a skipper cost?

The skipper's fee is paid separately from the boat, and guests usually cover the skipper's meals. Send me your dates and I'll give you an exact figure.

Do you cook and clean?

A skipper is responsible for the boat and your safety, not for cooking or cleaning guest cabins. If you'd like that, ask the charter company about a hostess.

Planning a week at sea?

Tell me your dates, the boat or charter company if you already have one, how many of you there are and what kind of week you'd like. I'll reply with my availability.
