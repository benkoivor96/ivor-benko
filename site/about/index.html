My story

I'm a skipper from Zagreb. I've skied my whole life and became a licensed ski instructor. While studying at the Faculty of Kinesiology, where I specialised in sailing and water sports, I discovered sailing in 2019 and fell in love with it straight away.

Since then I've sailed small boat classes like the Laser Radial, Optimist and Laser Stratos, windsurfed, raced in regattas, worked as a sailing instructor for the faculty on Murter and sailed big boats along the Adriatic. In 2023 I turned the hobby into my profession, and I've worked as a captain and skipper for private guests and charters ever since.

I know the Adriatic weather well, and I've been organising sports activities for more than ten years, from skiing and windsurfing to fitness, acrobatics and breakdance. People tell me I'm communicative, cheerful and organised, which is most of what you want from someone you share a boat with for a week.

Outside of sailing I build websites and apps and write, and in winter I'm back on the slopes.
